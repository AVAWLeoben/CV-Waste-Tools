CV Waste Studio v10.1 — Adaptive Datasets & Training Layout
========================================================

Before starting a dataset job (SETUP MODE):
  - The configuration form uses nearly the full workspace.
  - The shared activity log is a compact 2-line dock.
  - The form, workflow tabs, and Start button retain their original functionality.

After starting a training, split-only, or pre-annotation job (MONITOR MODE):
  - The configuration form is hidden automatically.
  - The activity area expands to occupy the full workspace.
  - Live batch progress, Ultralytics epoch charts, and recent log lines remain visible.
  - The active job status and Stop running job button remain accessible.
  - Show settings restores a fully scrollable configuration form while keeping
    the progress indicator, status, log, and Stop button visible in a short dock.
  - Hide settings returns to the live monitor.
  - Show log / Hide log and Show graphs / Hide graphs continue to work.
  - With the graphs hidden, the text log expands to use available room.

When the job ends:
  - The form-first setup layout and compact log return.
  - Successful training still opens the Training Results page when available.
  - Switching to the Training Results page still uses the full workspace.

No changes to Ultralytics training, the dataloader, model parameters, disk cache,
annotation/audit functions, environment setup, or launchers.

Verification:
  - 58 automated tests passed under Xvfb (18 additional subtests).
  - Tkinter setup/monitor/settings-toggle/completion/results navigation checked.
  - Py_compile passed for the launcher and training-related modules.
  - Native Windows desktop display and GPU training not exercised here.
